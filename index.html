Who am I

Business foundation, technical depth on the way.

From business to Data & Cloud

I studied Marketing & Market Research at the University of Málaga and did ThePowerMBA, and I've spent the last few years working in sales, analytics and digital marketing. Along the way I realized that what I enjoy most is the data behind the decisions, so now I'm building real technical skills in Python, data and cloud.

Today I'm in the first year of the Tridente program at UTAMED: Web Application Development (DAW), then a specialization in AI & Machine Learning and a degree in Computer Engineering. At the same time I work as a tech recruitment consultant at QGROUP for the Dutch market, talking every day with engineers and hiring managers about stacks, systems and what a team really needs.

My focus is using these tools to solve real operational problems in aerospace, industry and B2B platforms.

It all started as a kid, looking up at the sky and talking about the cosmos with my dad. Rockets, space and New Space have fascinated me ever since.

Vision & values

Science and technology are moving faster than ever. AI and quantum computing are changing the world around us, and I can't stop following it. People aren't always at their best, but what we've managed to build as humans amazes me. That's why I'm sure my life will revolve around technology and the progress of humanity.

I value honesty, respect and loyalty, and I can't stand lying or injustice. My goal is to use every bit of the intelligence God has given me to do things right and to fight injustice wherever I can.

Starting from zero in tech, I explored widely before committing to a single label. I wanted to understand what Computer Science actually contains before deciding where to go deep.
